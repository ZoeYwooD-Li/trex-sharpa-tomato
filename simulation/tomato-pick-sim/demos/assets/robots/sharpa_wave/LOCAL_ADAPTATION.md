# Bilateral Wave assets

Left assets and licenses are copied from LycheeSim `sharpa_wave_official`;
see PROVENANCE.md for pinned upstream revisions. Right assets are copied from
LycheeSim `demos/assets/sharpa_scissors/right_sharpa_wave`; the local snapshot
hashes are recorded in snapshot_sha256.json. The right source revision was
not recorded by that source directory and is not asserted here.

`right_sharpa_wave_with_flange.xml` adds the left model's flange/wrist meshes
and translates the right palm and finger roots by +0.0295 m in Z to match
the left mounting stack. Authored right joint axes and mesh chirality remain
unchanged. Source XML is preserved alongside the derived XML.
